V6 uses a NASA LRO-derived lunar mosaic plus a local backend that queries NASA PDS GDS. LOLA elevation and a derived local slope estimate are sampled from NASA LOLA observations. Diviner brightness temperature and solar geometry are used when observations are available. Earth analogue matching and landing physics remain prototype modules.

PRESENTATION MODE

5-step mission walkthrough

  1. Select a lunar site on the 3D globe.
  2. Review NASA LOLA / Diviner observations.
  3. Inspect hazards and the Earth analogue.
  4. Run the descent simulation.
  5. Show the touchdown result and data sources.
